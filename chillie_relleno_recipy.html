How to Cook NM Style Chile Rellenos

Fire-roasted green chile pods stuffed with thick block cheese, nestled in a baking dish, generously smothered in homemade baked red chile sauce, and baked until bubbling, molten, and rich.

Prep Time 30 mins
Bake Time 18 mins
Method Oven Baked

🇿🇦 The South African Produce Solution:
  • The Pod & Heat Bridge: South African supermarkets only sell sweet green bell peppers (0 heat), jalapeños, and African bird's eye chillies (100,000+ Scoville — way too hot to stuff!). Char green bell peppers as your stuffing shell, and tuck 1 thin matchstick of roasted jalapeño inside.
  • The 80/20 Red Sauce Secret: South African store-bought chili powder is intensely hot. To get the rich red Hatch depth without scorching your palate, use an 80% mild paprika to 20% chili powder ratio!
  • The Beans: Pinto beans are hard to find in SA. Use local red kidney beans (or black beans) as the ideal creamy substitute.

Step-by-Step Cooking Method

Follow each step in synchronization with the documentary video

1

Roast and Sweat the Chiles

Roast whole Hatch green chiles over high open flame or propane roaster tumbler until the skin blisters completely black and separates. Immediately transfer the hot pods into a plastic bag or covered bowl to steam for 15–20 minutes. The trapped steam loosens the skin so it slides off effortlessly.

2

Peel, Slit & Stuff with Cheese

Gently slide the charred skin off with your fingers—do not rinse under water, which washes away the smoky essential oils! Leave the stem attached as a dipping handle. Cut a clean 2-inch slit down the belly and gently scrape out the placenta and seeds. Insert thick spears of cheese (plus optional smoked turkey or shredded beef).

3

Simmer & Blend the Red Sauce

Husk fresh green tomatillos and rinse the sticky residue. In a skillet, combine halved tomatillos, oven-baked dried red chile pods, garlic, cumin, and chicken broth. Simmer for 10 minutes until tomatillos soften. Transfer to a blender and puree until velvety smooth.

4

Assemble in Baking Dish & Smother

Preheat your oven or toaster oven to 375°F (190°C). Pour a generous 1/2-inch layer of your homemade warm red sauce across the bottom of a baking dish. Lay the cheese-stuffed chile pods side-by-side in the sauce. Spoon additional red sauce over each pod, leaving just the stems showing. Top each chile with an extra handful of shredded cheese.

5

Bake Until Bubbling & Molten (15–18 Mins)

Bake uncovered at 375°F (190°C) for 15 to 18 minutes. The red sauce will bubble and reduce around the chiles while the cheese inside melts completely into an elastic, molten core and the top cheese turns golden brown.

6

Plating with Mexican Rice & Beans

Use a wide spatula to transfer the smothered baked rellenos directly onto warm plates. Spoon pan juices and extra sauce over top. Serve alongside fluffy Mexican rice and warm seasoned beans. Cut through the center for that epic molten cheese pull!

Common Questions & Pro Tips

Q: Why bake the rellenos directly in the red sauce?

Baking smothered rellenos (*Chile Rellenos al Horno*) allows the roasted chiles to absorb the smoky, tangy juices of the red sauce as they bake. The cheese melts into a fondue-like core without needing deep frying or raw egg batter, making it naturally gluten-free and lighter.

Q: Can I add smoked turkey or meat?

Yes! As shown in the video, shredding leftover smoked turkey or seasoned shredded beef and tucking it into the belly alongside the cheese adds amazing savory depth.

Q: Why the 80% Paprika / 20% Chili Powder ratio for South Africa?

South African supermarket chili powder is typically made with high-Scoville cayenne and African chillies. Using 100% chili powder would make the sauce scorchingly hot and overpower the delicate cheese. Blending 80% sweet paprika (for earthy red body) with 20% chili powder hits the sweet spot of authentic Hatch heat!
