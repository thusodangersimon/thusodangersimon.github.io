How to Cook NM Style Chile Rellenos

Fire-roasted green chile pods stuffed with molten cheese, blanketed in a traditional airy egg-white soufflé cloud, fried to golden perfection, and smothered in homemade baked red sauce.

Prep Time 35 mins
Cook Time 25 mins
Yield 6 Rellenos
Heat Level Medium (1.5k SHU)

Step-by-Step Cooking Method

Follow each step in synchronization with the documentary video

1

Roast and Sweat the Chiles

Roast whole Hatch green chiles over high open flame or propane roaster tumbler until the skin blisters completely black and separates. Immediately transfer the hot pods into a plastic bag or covered bowl to steam for 15–20 minutes. The trapped steam loosens the skin so it slides off effortlessly.

2

Peel, Slit & Stuff (Stem Intact)

Gently slide the charred skin off with your fingers—do not rinse under water, which washes away the smoky essential oils! Leave the stem attached as a dipping handle. Cut a clean 2-inch slit down the belly and gently scrape out the placenta and seeds. Insert thick spears of Asadero cheese. Pinch the seam closed.

3

Simmer & Blend the Red Sauce

Husk fresh green tomatillos and rinse the sticky residue. In a skillet, combine halved tomatillos, oven-baked dried red chile pods, garlic, cumin, and chicken broth. Simmer for 10 minutes until tomatillos soften. Transfer to a blender and puree until velvety smooth. Return to the pan and keep warm.

4

Whip the Soufflé Cloud Batter

Separate 4 large eggs. In a spotless glass or copper bowl, beat egg whites with 1/4 tsp salt until stiff, glossy peaks form. In a separate bowl, lightly whisk the yolks. Gently fold the yolks into the whipped whites with a silicone spatula in slow figure-eight motions, preserving the airy trapped bubbles.

5

Dredge, Coat & Pan-Fry at 375°F (190°C)

Heat 1.5 inches of neutral frying oil to 375°F (190°C). Lightly dust each stuffed chile in seasoned all-purpose flour (this gives the batter traction). Holding the stem handle, lower into the egg cloud batter until completely encased, then transfer directly into hot oil. Fry for 2 minutes per side until puffed and golden brown. Drain on paper towels.

6

Plating & Grand Cheese Pull

Pool a generous ladle of warm homemade red sauce across the plate. Lay two golden rellenos on top. Spoon additional red sauce across the center, leaving the crispy ends visible. Serve with fresh Mexican rice, pinto beans, and a sprinkle of cheese. Cut through the center for that epic molten cheese stretch!

Common Questions & Pro Tips

Q: Why did my batter slide off in the oil?

The chile pods were either too wet or you skipped the flour dusting. Pat the chiles completely dry with paper towels, and dust with flour right before dipping into the egg cloud.

Q: Can I bake them instead of frying?

Authentic New Mexican rellenos require hot oil to flash-cook the delicate egg white soufflé before it deflates. If you want oven finish, fry them for 90 seconds to set the crust, then transfer to a 375°F (190°C) oven for 5 minutes to melt the cheese.

Q: What is "Christmas Style"?

In New Mexico, when your server asks "Red or Green?", asking for "Christmas" means you get half your plate smothered in red chile sauce and the other half in green chile sauce!
